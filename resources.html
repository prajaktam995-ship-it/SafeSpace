📚 Resources & Information

Knowledge and support for your journey to safety

Understanding Abuse

  • Physical Abuse: Hitting, slapping, choking, restraining, or any unwanted physical contact
  • Emotional Abuse: Name-calling, threats, intimidation, isolation from friends/family
  • Financial Abuse: Controlling money, preventing work, hiding assets, ruining credit
  • Sexual Abuse: Any unwanted sexual activity, including within marriage
  • Digital Abuse: Monitoring devices, social media harassment, sharing private images

Online Safety Tips

  • Use Private Browsing: Always use incognito/private mode
  • Clear History: Delete browsing history, downloads, and cache
  • Use Public Computers: Libraries, schools, or internet cafes
  • Check for Spyware: Look for unfamiliar apps or unusual battery drain
