⚖️ Legal Rights & Information

Understanding your legal options and protections

Restraining Orders / Protection Orders

  • What it does: Legal document ordering abuser to stay away from you
  • Who can get one: Current/former partners, family members
  • Where to apply: Local courthouse
  • Cost: Usually free or low-cost
  • Violation: Breaking a restraining order is a crime

Your Rights

  • Right to Safety: No one has the right to hurt you
  • Right to Leave: You can leave an abusive relationship
  • Right to Report: You can report abuse to police
  • Right to Medical Care: Seek medical attention for injuries
  • Right to Support: Access to victim services and counseling

Getting Legal Help

  • Legal Aid Organizations: Free legal services for low-income individuals
  • Domestic Violence Programs: Often have legal advocates
  • Pro Bono Attorneys: Lawyers providing free services
  • Court Self-Help Centers: Assistance with forms and procedures

Disclaimer: This information is general and not legal advice. Laws vary by state. Consult with a local attorney or legal advocate for advice specific to your situation.
