📋 Personal Safety Planning

Creating a plan can help you stay safer

Step 1: Safe Places to Go

Identify safe places where you can go in an emergency:

  • Friends or family members who will help
  • Local domestic violence shelter
  • Public places (library, store, restaurant)
  • Religious or community centers
  • Hospital or police station

Step 2: Important Documents

Gather copies of important documents and store them safely:

  • Driver's license and Social Security cards
  • Birth certificates (yours and children's)
  • Marriage certificate, divorce papers
  • Insurance policies and medical records
  • Bank statements and credit cards

Step 3: Emergency Kit

Prepare a bag with essentials:

  • Cash and credit cards
  • Change of clothes
  • Medications
  • Keys (house, car, work)
  • Phone charger
  • Important phone numbers
